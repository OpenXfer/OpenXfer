# About using generative AI

This file says how I used generative AI tools in preparing this repository and the related grant materials, in line with NLnet / NGI Zero's policy on transparency.

## Tools used

- Claude (Anthropic).

## What I used AI for

- **Translating and tightening text.** Croatian is my first language. I used the AI to help translate my notes and analysis into English and to keep the writing within the limits of the application form.
- **Looking things up.** Verifying EU rules (ViDA), classifying B2B e-invoicing models country by country, and comparing with existing work (EN16931, France's AFNOR XP Z12-013, Peppol). I cross-checked the facts against public sources.
- **Restructuring older notes.** Reorganising earlier internal documents into the form they are in here.

## Where the content comes from

- My own work and my own domain knowledge from more than 25 years building ERP and accounting software in Croatia.
- My earlier public analyses (the 2018 letter to the Ministry of Finance and the follow-up meeting with the Croatian Tax Authority, and the 2025 analysis of the missing ERP-to-intermediary layer).
- Public sources (the European Commission's eInvoicing country pages and other reputable sources for industry context).

## Who is responsible

I am. All the design choices, all the technical decisions, all the scope definitions — those are mine, and I went through the output to check it. The AI helped me write faster and in better English. It did not think for me.

The same applies to anything that comes later under funded work. I will use AI tools where they speed up engineering, but I will always be the one reviewing, deciding and taking responsibility for the result. If the funder requires more detailed AI usage logs for funded deliverables, those will be kept.
